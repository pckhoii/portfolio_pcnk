Mission 01 / Marketing Analytics

Marketing Spend Performance Analysis

A mission to find whether marketing growth was powered by true efficiency or simply by a larger budget. The work turns campaign data into a decision layer for budget allocation, channel optimization and performance storytelling.

Python Pandas Power BI ROAS Budget Optimization
View Dashboard GitHub Repo

Question

The signal to investigate

Revenue was moving up, but the core question was less obvious: were campaigns becoming more efficient, or was the business buying growth by increasing spend? Looking only at revenue, clicks or orders could create a false positive.

System

The analytical orbit

I cleaned and prepared campaign data with Python, modeled performance metrics such as revenue, cost, ROAS, ROMI and conversion rate, then built a Power BI dashboard for channel, category, platform and campaign analysis.

Dashboard

The mission control panel

The dashboard is designed as a navigation layer: start from spend and revenue, drill into channels and campaigns, then compare efficiency metrics before deciding where budget should scale, pause or be reworked.

Signals

What the data revealed

Signal 01

Influencer quality stood out

Influencer campaigns produced strong revenue with comparatively lower cost, making them a better candidate for controlled scaling than broad paid social.

Signal 02

Social volume was not enough

Social generated large traffic and spend, but weaker monetization suggested the need to audit targeting, creative fatigue and post-click intent.

Signal 03

Growth was spend-heavy

High impressions and clicks did not translate into equally strong conversion efficiency, so the business needed to separate reach from return.

Decision Layer

Recommended actions

Scale: Prioritize influencer campaigns with better return signals and stronger conversion quality.

Optimize: Rework social campaigns around audience segmentation, creative strategy and conversion intent.

Re-evaluate: Treat high-order or high-click campaigns carefully when ROAS and ROMI do not support the same story.

Monitor: Use the dashboard as a recurring decision layer instead of a one-time reporting artifact.
